Momentum Setup Screener

Ranks a universe of daily bars against Kristjan Kullamägi's three setups and returns, for every candidate, why it qualifies and exactly where the entry and the hard stop go. The rules behind each test are documented in the playbook (local copy).

A bundled 25-symbol universe of synthetic daily bars covering June 2025 – September 2026, built to exercise every branch of the screen: live breakouts, an episodic pivot, a parabolic short, and instructive rejects. Move the as of date back to replay any session — 2 July 2026 for the MEMQ breakout, 22 July for the NRGX episodic pivot, 10 August for the ADVX short.

Nothing here is investment advice, and synthetic bars prove nothing about the market. Import real data to screen real candidates.

Screens the whole US market straight from yfinance — no CSV in the middle. A small local server resolves the constituent list, downloads daily bars and applies the liquidity gates; this page screens what comes back. Start it from the repository:

pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
python3 tools/server.py --open

The whole universe is ~3,500 tickers and takes several minutes on the first run; the result is cached for the day, so a second fetch is instant. Start with a cap of 300 to see it work end to end. Yahoo rate-limits aggressively — if batches come back empty, raise the delay in tools/server.py --sleep.

Daily bars, one row per symbol per session, newest or oldest first — the order does not matter. Header must include date and close; symbol, open, high, low and volume are used when present. At least 130 sessions per symbol are needed (the 6-month relative-strength lookback). A symbol named SPY, QQQ or IWM is used as the index for the regime filter instead of being screened.

Risk inputs drive the share count; filter inputs drive eligibility. His own numbers are the defaults: 0.5% risk per trade, 20% maximum position, a 3.5% ADR floor.

Candidates

Screening logic implements rules published by Kristjan Kullamägi (qullamaggie.com): ADR-capped stops, opening-range entries, partials after 3–5 sessions, a 10/20-day moving-average trail.

A 25–30% win rate means long losing streaks are normal. Position sizing, not stock picking, is what keeps a losing streak survivable. Not investment advice.
